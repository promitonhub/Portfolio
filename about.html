01 / SUMMARYWHAT I WORK ON

Research that leads somewhere.

My experience spans B2B demand generation, account and opportunity research, cold calling, email and LinkedIn outreach, whitepaper production, CRM workflows, RFP and proposal support, and event and brand communications.

In a confidential B2B technology-services environment, I helped connect these activities rather than treating every channel as a separate campaign. The common thread is turning context into communication that is relevant, organised, and useful to the next person in the process.
