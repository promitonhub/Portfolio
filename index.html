03 / APPROACHA SIMPLE WORKING METHODPROCESS NOTES — 01

      

        
RESEARCH → COORDINATE → HAND OFF
Find the signal.
Make it useful.
The working method behind the outreach, content and proposal systems shown above.

        

          
01UNDERSTAND⌕
Find a buying signal.
Start with a market or account, then look for funding, budget announcements, system-change needs, procurement activity or another reason to engage.
START WITH WHY

          
02ORGANISE▤
Keep the channels connected.
Organise research, calls, email, LinkedIn activity and follow-up around a shared prospect record and communication history.
FIND THE PATTERN

          
03COMMUNICATE↗
Make the outcome clear.
Turn the work into a brief, proposal, presentation or solution people can act on.
MAKE IT ACTIONABLE
